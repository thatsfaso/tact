Stories a blind child can read with their own hands.

Tact turns a spoken or typed idea into a short story, writes it in braille, adds a raised picture and gives you a file to print at home on a 3D printer.

Make a story Source code

Why it exists

A sighted child can ask for a story about anything and hear it that same evening. A blind child usually waits for someone to transcribe a book, and only the books someone already chose ever get transcribed. Braille books cost many times more than print and take weeks to make.

Tact makes the story the child asked for, on the same day, as a page they can keep and read again.

How it works

  1. 01

    Say or type an idea

    One sentence is enough, in Italian or English: a fox who guards a castle.

  2. 02

    Tact builds the page

    It writes a short story, translates it into Grade 1 braille and picks a matching tactile picture.

  3. 03

    Print it at home

    Download the STL file and print it in TPU or PETG on any FDM 3D printer with a 220 mm bed. Each story fills two cards.

Shaped by readers

The page has changed because of people who read braille. A teacher of students with visual impairments reviewed the first version and advised against short braille lines. Then a blind reader printed a page and told us what did not work: the picture was too small to recognise and sat in too much empty plastic.

Today every picture is a simple filled silhouette in the top-right corner, only the first four lines are a little shorter, and the braille is the first thing the printer lays down.

Privacy and openness

There is no account and nothing to install. The story is written by a hosted model that receives only your one-line idea or, when that is not available, by a small model running in your own browser. The braille and the 3D file are made on your device.

Who made it

Tact was created by Iliano Fasolino, a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Milan. It is still a prototype. If you print a page, read one or teach braille, your feedback shapes the next version.
